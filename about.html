Compalgo's romantic compatibility algorithm calculates the Romantic Compatibility Score between two individuals.

We have built the algorithm by observing recurrent patterns in relationships that have proven to hold in the long run.

You are welcome to test the validity of this algorithm for yourself and do your own tests among family members and friends. You can also test public celebrities, whose information you will find on Wikipedia. You will often, but not always, find celebrities' birth order under the section "Early life".

Why should you use the Compalgo compatibility calculator?

  • To help you choose between various dates, based on science. You still need to listen to your heart, guts and brain.
  • So that you don't lose time, energy, or money on incompatible dates.
  • To prove to 'stalkers' that you are not compatible with them.
  • To convince you to stop clinging onto people you simply are not compatible with.
  • It helps you explain why seemingly very different characters can still be a good match.
  • To minimize the chances of divorce.
  • Most of the science behind Compalgo is applicable to other kinds of relationships too.

Compalgo's romantic compatibility algorithm was created by Henrik Hillerström, who was inspired by his fictive characters in The Grooters book series.
