Data usage and privacy

When using Compalgo, you are asked to provide certain information such as names, birth dates and birth order. We want to assure you that:

  • The compatibility calculation runs entirely in your own browser.
  • Nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere – not to us and not to anyone else.
  • We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous visit statistics, such as how many people visit which pages. Google Analytics never receives the names, dates or scores from the calculator.
  • Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes.
