200+

For a successful long-term relationship, aim for a Compatibility Score of at least 200 points. However, love is complex, and there's more to consider beyond just the numbers.

Important factors to consider

Emotional cement

This is a crucial factor that binds couples together through shared experiences:

  • Positive bonds: common school memories, travel experiences, interests, or languages.
  • Strong negative experiences (unless overwhelming): similar life challenges like family difficulties or personal hardships.

If you have strong emotional cement, add 100 points to your Compatibility Score!

Common goals and values

Having shared goals beyond self-interest can strengthen relationships, even with lower compatibility scores. However, conflicting core values ('deal-breakers') can challenge even high-scoring couples unless resolved through communication and adaptation.
